# Video sources

Stats shown in the opening of `video/out/savingsdetective.mp4`.

1. **37% of US adults could not cover a $400 emergency expense with cash or its equivalent.** Federal Reserve Board, Economic Well-Being of U.S. Households (SHED) 2024, published May 2025. https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/report-economic-well-being-us-households.htm. The exact figure was quoted from memory and the page returned an error during the build; verify before public use.
2. **47.7 million Americans had a package stolen in the last year, worth $7.7 billion.** Security.org package theft annual report, updated Oct 28, 2026. https://www.security.org/package-theft/annual-report/. The page notes these are survey extrapolations, not a direct count.
3. **Median household income in Ohio: about $69,000.** US Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2023 1-year estimates, table S1901. https://data.census.gov/table/ACSST1Y2023.S1901?g=040XX00US39. Rounded; not re-fetched during the build.

Household math (stated assumption, not shown as a sourced stat): the demo household placed 308 Amazon orders in 2022. If 2 to 3 percent of orders carry a price drop inside a window or a late guaranteed delivery, that is 6 to 9 claims a year at the demo's average of about $25 each, roughly $150 to $230 a year.
